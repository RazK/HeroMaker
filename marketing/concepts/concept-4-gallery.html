How it works

Four steps, one of them yours.

01

Snap the drawing

Lay the page flat, take a photo with your phone, and upload it — crayon, pencil, marker or paint all work.

02

We render it

The drawing is interpreted into a clean full-body character that keeps your child's shapes and colours.

03

We build it in 3D

That character becomes a real 3D model and is rigged with a humanoid skeleton so it can be animated.

04

They play as it

It appears in the Playground, ready for the webcam — and it is yours to download and keep.

For parents

The plain terms, in four lines.

No advertising

There are no ads in HeroMaker and nothing is sold to your child inside the game.

You own what they make

Every hero is yours. Download the file, keep it, use it elsewhere — it is a standard VRM.

Drawings train nothing

Your child's artwork is used to build their hero and for nothing else. We do not train models on it.

Delete it all, any time

One button removes the drawings, the heroes and the account, from our servers and our backups.
