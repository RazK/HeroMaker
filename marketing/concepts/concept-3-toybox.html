How it works

Four steps. You do one of them.

The rest is our pipeline, and it takes about six minutes while they go and find the next drawing.

1

Snap the drawing

Flatten it on a table and take one photo with your phone. Crayon, felt tip, pencil, all fine.

2

We render the character

Our model reads the drawing and paints a clean, full-colour version of exactly that character.

3

We build and rig it in 3D

It becomes a solid model with a 22-bone skeleton, so arms, legs and head all move properly.

4

They play as it

Open the Playground, wave at the webcam, and their hero dances the moves they make.

For parents

Screen time that leaves something behind.

You are handing us a picture your child made. Here is exactly what happens to it, in plain words.

Photo: parent and child at the laptop, hero on screen

  • No ads, ever

    Nothing is sold to your child inside the product. You buy credits, that is the whole business.

  • You own what they make

    The drawing and the hero are yours. Download the 3D file and keep it, with or without us.

  • Drawings never train anything

    We use your child's artwork to build your child's hero. It does not go into any training set.

  • Delete it all in one click

    Photos, heroes, account. One button removes everything, and webcam video never leaves the device.
