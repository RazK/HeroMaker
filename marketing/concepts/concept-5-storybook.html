How it works

Four small steps, one big moment.

Nothing to install, nothing to configure. You hold the phone; we do the rest.

1

Snap the drawing

Flatten it on the table and take one photo — crayon, marker or biro all work.

2

We paint it up

Our renderer reads the drawing and turns it into a clean, full-colour character.

3

We build it in 3D

That character becomes a 3D model with a proper skeleton inside it.

4

They play as it

Webcam on, music up — your child moves and the hero moves with them.
