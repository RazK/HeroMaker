How it works

Four steps. Your child does the first and the last one.

1

Snap the drawing

A phone photo of the paper is enough — crayon, felt tip, biro, on a table or the fridge door.

2

We build it in 3D

The character is redrawn cleanly, given volume, and turned into a real mesh that holds its own shape.

3

We rig it to move

A 22-bone humanoid skeleton goes in, so any dance we have ever recorded plays on it correctly.

4

Play as it

Open the Playground and your hero mirrors your child through the webcam, live, in the browser.

For the grown-up paying

Four things we think you should know before your child's drawing goes anywhere.

No ads, ever

Nothing is sold to your child, and nothing is sold about them. You pay for credits; that is the whole business.

You own what your child makes

The drawing is theirs and so is the hero. Download the 3D file whenever you like and keep it.

Drawings never train anything

Your child's artwork is used to build your child's hero. It is not added to any training set, ours or anyone else's.

Delete it all in one click

Remove a single hero or wipe the whole account. Webcam video stays in the browser and is never uploaded.
