# Held back: photos with an identifiable person

**These files are NOT committed.** They are excluded by `.gitignore` one level
up, because this repository publishes to GitHub Pages — anything committed here
is effectively public.

Every image in this folder shows at least one identifiable face. Four of them
show identifiable **children**:

- `kids-watching-hero-dance.jpg` — two boys, faces lit by the screen
- `group-kids-holding-drawings.jpg` — around eight children holding their drawings
- `group-with-hero-gallery-screen.jpg` — the same group, wider
- `families-at-the-heromaker-booth.jpg` — families at the booth

The other four show identifiable adults only.

## Before any of these goes on the website

The product owner must confirm they hold permission from each person shown —
and from a parent or guardian for each child. That is a decision for a human,
not for an agent, so nothing here is used until it is made.

## You may not need them

`../CONTACT-SHEET.md` lists a recommended safe set with zero identifiable
faces that covers every slot on the landing page. The only genuinely
hard-to-replace images are the two group shots, which are the "look how many
kids did this" social proof.
