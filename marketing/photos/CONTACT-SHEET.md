# HeroMaker marketing photos — contact sheet

23 images selected from two shared Google Photos albums (134 real photos total).
Everything here is a re-encoded JPEG under 600 KB; originals stay in the shared
albums.

## Where they came from

- **Album 1 — 47 photos.** Daytime public event, Hanukkah 2025, at a children's
  hospital atrium (Schneider Children's Medical Center). Bright daylight, lots
  of real kids drawing, real kids playing the webcam game on a big portrait
  screen. **This is the useful album for a kids' landing page.**
- **Album 2 — 87 photos.** Evening adult art-event installation (Museum of
  Italian Jewish Art, Jerusalem — "שאון חורף"). Dim tungsten light, adult
  visitors, a wall papered with drawings. Atmospheric, but almost no children
  and mostly too dark for a hero slot. Used here only for drawing-texture and
  workshop-mood shots.

## ⚠️ Faces and consent

Rows flagged **FACE VISIBLE: yes** contain at least one identifiable person.
Rows marked **yes (child)** contain an identifiable **child** — the product
owner must confirm written parental permission before any of those go on a
public website. Rows marked **yes (adult)** still need the adult's permission,
but carry no child-privacy risk.

The four slots below each have at least one **faceless** option, and those are
the recommended defaults.

## Notes

- Several of these are frames pulled from videos in the albums. Requesting the
  image with a `-no` suffix on the size parameter (`=w2048-no`) strips Google's
  play-button overlay, which is how these were captured cleanly.
- Album 1 has more usable footage than is selected here — particularly more
  angles of kids at the big screen — if a different crop or aspect ratio is
  needed later.
