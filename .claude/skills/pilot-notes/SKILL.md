# Pilot notes

At our traffic we learn from watching people, not from A/B tests
(`docs/product/OPERATING.md`). A pilot is only useful if we decide in advance
what it must answer.

## Before

Write `docs/product/pilots/P-NNN-<slug>.md` with:
- Date, place, who runs it, how many children, the door tag (`?via=`).
- **Three questions** it must answer, each tied to a hypothesis id
  (`docs/product/HYPOTHESES.md`) or a money moment. Example: "Do parents ask
  how to keep the hero?" (H-0xx, "I want to keep this").
- What to count by hand (how many asked for the link, scanned the QR,
  asked the price, waited until the end).
- Raz's yes, if it shows anything new to children.

## During (the person on site, or Raz dictating after)

What people **do and say**, not what we hope: where they wait, what they
point at, what they ask, who asks for the link or a price, where they leave.
Quote words exactly, without who said them.

**Never** a child's name, photo, face or drawing in the notes, the repo or
any message. Ages as a band only (4-6, 7-9, 10-12).

## After

One line per question: what we now believe, and how sure (how many people).
Update the hypotheses it touched. Add any decision to `DECISIONS.md` once
Raz makes it. Mention it in the next Sunday review.
