# Money check

Raz's rule: we do not spend time on things that do not bring money, and the
agents steer him when an idea is not right. Run this before you build.

Read `docs/product/MONEY_MOMENTS.md` and `docs/product/OPERATING.md` if they
are not in your context.

Answer five questions, one line each:

1. **Which money moment does it serve?** The reveal, the child's reaction,
   "I want to keep this", the gift, home from class. None: say so.
2. **How soon does it bring money?** Before Schneider (10 Dec), this month,
   later, never.
3. **Which number does it move?** Paid heroes per week, or one step of the
   funnel to it (visit, start, painting seen, purchase, share).
4. **What is the smallest version** that tells us if it works? Reuse what
   exists before building new (look at the code first).
5. **What does it cost?** Days of work, provider money, Raz's time.

Then a verdict: **do it**, **do a smaller version**, **later** (write it
down, with the trigger to revisit) or **no** (say why, in one line).

Not money moments, unless they unblock one: more games, polish for its own
sake, gallery tests, animations for their own sake, tooling nobody asked for.

Bugs that break the money path (sign-in, checkout, credits, a hero failing)
skip this check: fix them.

Tell Raz the verdict first, in simple English, then the five lines only if
he asks.
