# Films: Class and Birthday (outlines, for the PM)

Two more buyers, the same building blocks as the family film (the drawing,
the reveal, coming alive). Outlines first: the PM checks the money and what the
product really does before either becomes a full script.

**Check first: the group finale.** Both end with every child's hero dancing
together on one big screen. That is the "class stage"
(`GET /api/v1/classes/{id}/stage`, prototype on `claude/proto-hero-class`). We
show it only if it is in the product when the film runs; otherwise the finale
is the class gallery projected, every hero in it, which exists.

## Class ("Every child takes a hero home")

**Who pays:** an art teacher, per seat ($1.25, H-027). **Door:** `ad-teacher`.
**Second buyer:** the parents, through Show your parents (H-029).

1. A classroom: the teacher projects the class PIN; twenty kids at tables, drawing.
2. Close-ups: three very different drawings (a cat, a robot, a girl with wings).
3. The projected screen: heroes appear one by one in the class gallery; the room reacts.
4. The finale: every hero on the big screen together; the kids dance with them.
5. Home, that evening: a child shows her mum the hero on the phone (Show your parents).
6. End card: "One hero for every child in your class. No accounts." Run a class.

Cast: AI teacher, AI children. Never a real school, never a real child.

## Birthday ("The party everyone takes home")

**Who pays:** the parent hosting the party (H-026: $20+ for every guest).
**Door:** `ad-birthday`.

1. A living room with balloons; eight kids on the floor around paper, drawing.
2. The birthday girl's dad photographs the drawings, one by one.
3. The TV: the heroes appear, one by one; each kid shouts when theirs comes.
4. The finale: all the heroes dance together on the TV; the kids dance with them.
5. Going home: each guest leaves with a party bag and their hero on a parent's phone.
6. End card: "A party they take home." Make a hero.

Cast: all AI-generated.
