# Film: Family ("An afternoon that comes alive")

**Who pays:** a parent, for something to do with their child after school that
ends with a thing the child made. **Door:** `ad-family`. **Money moments:** 1
(the reveal) and 2 (the reaction). **Format:** 9:16 first (Raz prefers it),
then 4:5 and 16:9. 30 s, with a 15 s cut.

Cast, all AI-generated, never real: **Maya** (7) and **Mom**. Nothing says or
suggests they are customers.

## The story in one line

A rainy afternoon, a bored kid, a drawing, and Mom watching her daughter's
drawing come alive in their living room.

Every shot holds long enough to be understood: at least 2.5 s after any
