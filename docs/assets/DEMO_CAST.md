# The demo cast: our poster heroes

A small, fixed set of heroes we reuse everywhere — ads, the master film, demo
prints, the framed drawings on a stand, the gallery's Editors' choice. One
consistent cast makes the brand recognisable and saves us re-vetting assets
every time (Raz, 2026-11-23).

**Violet Volt is cast member #1.** We want about three more "poster boys".

## What makes a good cast member

A drawing earns a place only if it is strong at EVERY stage:

1. **The drawing** reads as a child's, bold and clear: a closed outline, a
   head, two arms, two legs, a face. Busy scribbles and faint pencil do not
   paint or rig well.
2. **The painting** (openai_render) comes out charming and on-brand.
3. **The 3D hero** rigs and animates cleanly on the 22-bone humanoid (roughly
   upright, limbs separable) and looks good doing a dance, a flip, a wave.
4. **The printed toy** prints and takes apart well in a few colours (Nir's
   cost table): a stable base, few colour swaps, no spindly parts.
5. **It speaks to the audience** (kids 7-10 and their parents): a hero, an
   animal, a creature — a range across the cast, not four of the same.

A candidate is only confirmed once it has gone through all five, not by the
drawing alone.

## Rights (hard rule)

- Real children's drawings from an exhibition may be used in **ads, printed
  samples or any public showcase ONLY with the parent's consent.** Track the
  consent per drawing before any such use.
- For internal pipeline testing, a drawing may be used without that, but it
  never leaves the building without consent.
- The safe default for the ad/print poster cast is **our own house-made
  drawings** in the Violet style (no consent needed, fully ours). Violet is
  one of these. Keep consented real drawings for a separate "real heroes" wall.
