# Weekly Operating Review (30 minutes)

1. **5 min — Scoreboard:** user outcome, business KPI, quality guardrails; actual vs target.
2. **10 min — Evidence:** releases, experiments, customer conversations, what changed in belief.
3. **10 min — Decisions:** highest-impact bottleneck, dependencies, alternatives, what to stop.
4. **5 min — Commitments:** 1–3 outcomes for next week, owner, date, method of validation.
