# H-025: Givers want to give a hero, not make one

We believe at least 5% of Buy-sheet views from the gift and birthday doors will tap 'Give it as a gift' because the giver is not the one with the drawing.

**Why we think so.** A gift voucher is a real feature with real work. A fake door costs a button.

**How we'll know.** Fake door. Supported: 5% or more. Rejected: under 1%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** The fake door must answer honestly ('Not yet. Thanks for telling us.') and collect nothing. Needs a design approved by Raz.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
