# H-030: Teachers run a second class

We believe at least 30% of teachers who ran a class will create another within 30 days because a lesson that worked is repeated with the next group.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 30% or more. Rejected: under 10%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
