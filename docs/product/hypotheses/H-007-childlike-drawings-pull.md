# H-007: Heroes from young kids' drawings draw people in

We believe heroes made from drawings that clearly look like a young child's will be opened at least 1.5 times as often per impression as polished drawings at the same grid place, because a parent thinks 'my kid could do this'.

**Why we think so.** Raz curates the gallery. Knowing what pulls decides what he features.

**How we'll know.** A/B experiment (rides on E-001). Supported: child-like heroes 1.5x or more. Rejected: 1.0x or less. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** The label (child-like or polished) is an editorial judgment about a gallery hero, written in the experiment file. It is not data about the child.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
