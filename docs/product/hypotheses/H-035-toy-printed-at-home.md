# H-035: Toy buyers print at home

We believe at least 40% of toys made will have their print file downloaded within 7 days because families who choose a print file have a printer or know someone who does.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 40% or more. Rejected: under 15%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** A download is not a print. A short follow-up interview with 5 downloaders says whether it was printed.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
