# H-023: A shared hero brings a new maker

We believe visitors who arrive through a shared hero link will start making a hero in at least 8% of visits because they just saw a real child's hero move.

**Why we think so.** A shared hero is the best possible ad: it is somebody's own child's.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation by door. Supported: 8% or more. Rejected: under 3%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Today a shared link arrives as 'direct', indistinguishable from someone typing the address.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
