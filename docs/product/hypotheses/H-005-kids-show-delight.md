# H-005: Kids light up when their hero moves

We believe kids 4-9 who see their own hero move will show visible delight (smile, call someone over, ask for another) in at least 8 of 10 observed sessions because the drawing they made is now alive.

**Why we think so.** The Hanukkah event at Schneider Children's Hospital is the precedent: kids drawing, then playing on a big screen. That is anecdote, not a count.

**How we'll know.** Observed session (at home, a class, a stand). Supported: 8 of 10 sessions. Rejected: 5 or fewer of 10. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Cheap and fast. It also shows what the screen gets wrong, which no event can.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
