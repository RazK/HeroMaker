# H-038: Grown-ups draw heroes too

We believe at least 20% of first heroes from the ad door will be drawn by a grown-up, and they will buy at least as often as kid drawers, because a grown-up's doodle turned into a toy is a novelty gift.

**Why we think so.** If grown-ups are a real share, the ads and the gallery are speaking to the wrong person.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 20% or more. Rejected: under 5%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Asked after payment, so the share is of buyers, not of visitors.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
