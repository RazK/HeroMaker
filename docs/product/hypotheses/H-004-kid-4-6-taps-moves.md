# H-004: Kids 4-6 keep tapping moves

We believe kids 4-6 will tap at least 5 moves on their first ready hero in at least 60% of heroes because seeing their own drawing dance is the moment they came for.

**Why we think so.** Young kids read little and tap a lot (USER_STORIES). Moves are the one thing they can do alone.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation, plus 5 observed sessions. Supported: 60% or more of these heroes get 5+ moves. Rejected: under 30%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Until the age band exists, read it for all heroes and in observed sessions.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
