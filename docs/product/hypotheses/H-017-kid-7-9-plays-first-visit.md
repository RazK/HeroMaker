# H-017: Kids 7-9 play a game with their new hero

We believe kids 7-9 will start a game with their hero in the first visit after it is ready in at least 50% of heroes because playing with something they made is the point.

**Why we think so.** The funnel ends at a game (REQUIREMENTS). This says whether the game is reached.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 50% or more. Rejected: under 25%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.
