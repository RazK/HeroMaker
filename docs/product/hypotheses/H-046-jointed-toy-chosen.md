# H-046: Children choose the jointed toy over the statue

We believe more than half of the children who touch a toy will pick up the jointed one (arms that swing) before the one-piece statue, because a toy that moves is a toy and a statue is decoration.

**Why we think so.** The jointed toy costs about twice as much to print (₪135 against ₪72, `docs/toy/COST.md`). We should only pay for joints if children want them. This is a guess, not a measurement.

**How we'll know.** Observed session at Schneider: both toys sit on the table as display only (no price, no pitch, per P-001), and we tally which one a child picks up first, by age band. Supported: more than half pick up the jointed toy first. Rejected: under a third. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** It needs the test order from JLC3DP to arrive by 8 Dec (`docs/toy/order/README.md`). The same tally by age band is also a first look at H-037. Never a child's name, face or photo in the notes.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
