# H-029: Class heroes bring parents in

We believe at least 10% of class heroes will be kept by a parent through Show your parents within 7 days, and at least 2% of class households will buy within 30 days, because the child comes home talking about it.

**Why we think so.** This is how a class turns into paying households (class-sessions.md).

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation by door. Supported: 10% kept and 2% buy. Rejected: under 3% kept. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
