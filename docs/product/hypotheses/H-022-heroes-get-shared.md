# H-022: Families share their hero

We believe at least 20% of heroes made at home will be shared because a moving hero is something to show grandma.

**Why we think so.** Sharing is the free channel. The share sends the hero's own link.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 20% or more. Rejected: under 8%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
