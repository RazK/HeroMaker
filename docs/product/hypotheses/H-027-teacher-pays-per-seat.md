# H-027: Teachers pay for seats at $1.25

We believe at least 40% of teachers who create a class will pay for its seats at $0.50 + $1.25 a seat because one hero per child for less than a coffee is an easy yes.

**Why we think so.** A class is a different buyer with a different budget. It is also the referral engine (H-029).

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation by door, plus 5 teacher interviews. Supported: 40% or more pay. Rejected: under 15%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Class seats cannot be bought with money on production today (READINESS item 9). Whether the teacher pays personally or needs a school invoice is part of the interviews.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
