# H-033: A venue pays a flat fee for a stand

We believe at least 2 of 5 fair or museum organisers will say they would pay $150 or more a day for a HeroMaker stand because it is an attraction with a take-home.

**Why we think so.** The other way a stand pays: the venue, not the families.

**How we'll know.** Interview. Supported: 2 of 5 at $150+ a day. Rejected: 0 of 5. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Raz runs these or says yes to them. The $150 is a target we chose, not a market price.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
