# H-024: A gift is a common reason to make a hero

We believe at least 15% of first heroes will be answered 'A gift' in What's it for, because a child's drawing turned into a hero is a natural present.

**Why we think so.** If gifts are common, gift is a product, not a door.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 15% or more. Rejected: under 5%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Asked after payment, so it describes buyers only.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
