# H-037: Older kids pull the toy apart

We believe kids 10 and over will pull the toy apart at least twice as often per toy view as kids 4-6 because the pieces are a puzzle to them and a broken hero to the little ones.

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation. Supported: 2x or more. Rejected: 1x or less. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
