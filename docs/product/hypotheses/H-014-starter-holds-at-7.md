# H-014: Starter still sells at $7

We believe parents will buy Starter at $7 at least 75% as often as at $5, because the value is the child's reaction, not the credit count.

**Why we think so.** Margins are 30% to 39% today, under the 50% floor (READINESS section 4). A price rise is one of Raz's three options.

**How we'll know.** Price test (one price per week, not side by side), after 5 price interviews. Supported: 75% or more of the $5 rate. Rejected: under 50%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Sequential, not A/B: two prices for the same thing on the same day is unfair to buyers and small traffic cannot separate them anyway. Interviews first.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
