# H-031: A staffed stand makes 20 heroes an hour

We believe at a staffed fair or museum stand at least 30% of children who stop will make a hero and the stand will finish at least 20 heroes an hour because drawing takes two minutes and the rest runs on its own.

**Why we think so.** Fairs and museums are a door Raz named. Meshy's task slots cap a busy hour (READINESS section 5).

**How we'll know.** Observed session. Supported: 30% of those who stop make one, and 20 heroes an hour. Rejected: under 10%, or under 8 an hour. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
