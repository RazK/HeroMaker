# H-016: Kids wait the 8 minutes

We believe at least 50% of first heroes are watched to the end on the being-made screen, without leaving, because the two questions and the painting fill the wait.

**Why we think so.** Decides whether to invest in the wait (a game, a preview) or in calling them back (H-015).

**How we'll know.** Funnel observation, plus observed sessions. Supported: 50% or more. Rejected: under 25%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
