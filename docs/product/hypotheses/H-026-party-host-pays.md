# H-026: A birthday host pays for every kid at the party

We believe at least 5 of 20 parents planning a party will say they would pay $20 or more for every guest to make a hero, and at least 2 of the first 10 classes created from the birthday door will be parties, because it is an activity and a take-home in one.

**Why we think so.** A party is a class with cake: the class feature already does it.

**How we'll know.** Interview, then funnel observation by door. Supported: 5 of 20 say yes at $20+, and 2 of 10 classes are parties. Rejected: 2 or fewer of 20. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** Interviews are run by Raz or with his yes; nobody is contacted by the agent.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
