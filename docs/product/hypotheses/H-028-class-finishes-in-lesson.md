# H-028: Every kid in a class gets a hero in the lesson

We believe at least 90% of kids who join a class will have a ready hero inside a 45-minute lesson because the class flow skips sign-in and payment.

**Why we think so.** A teacher who loses a third of the class to errors never comes back.

**How we'll know.** Observed session in a class. Supported: 90% or more. Rejected: under 70%. Anything between is inconclusive. The thresholds are targets we chose, not measurements.

**Notes.** 30 heroes at once is above Meshy Pro's 10 task slots (READINESS section 5): the test is also a capacity test.

**Result.** Not tested yet.
